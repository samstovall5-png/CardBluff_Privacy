Card Bluff Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains how Card Bluff handles information.

Last Updated: July 6, 2026

Overview

Thank you for playing Card Bluff. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game does and does not collect.

Information We Collect

Card Bluff does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or messages.

The game may save basic game progress, preferences, and settings locally on your device. This information is used only so the game can work correctly and remember your choices.

Internet and Online Features

Card Bluff does not require users to create an account. If online features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information is used.

Third-Party Services

Card Bluff may be distributed through platforms such as Google Play. Those platforms may collect limited technical information, such as crash reports or device information, according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Card Bluff does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe information was collected by mistake, please contact us so the issue can be reviewed.

Data Security

Since Card Bluff does not collect personal information on its own servers, we do not store personal user data.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.
